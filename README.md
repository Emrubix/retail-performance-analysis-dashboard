# retail-performance-analysis-dashboard
End-to-end retail performance analysis using Excel to uncover customer behaviour, revenue drivers, staffing optimisation opportunities, marketing efficiency and premium customer retention risks.


## Project Overview

This project analyses retail transaction data to identify operational improvements, customer behaviour patterns and revenue opportunities.

The objective was to move beyond reporting numbers and answer key business questions:

- Are staffing levels aligned with customer demand?
- Does the membership programme create measurable value?
- Can marketing investment be improved through customer segmentation?
- Are premium customers at risk of revenue loss?

The analysis was completed using Microsoft Excel, including data cleaning, pivot tables, KPI analysis and dashboard development.

---

# Business Problem

Retail management identified several operational challenges:

- Different branches reported different peak trading periods.
- Marketing campaigns were applied broadly despite differences in customer behaviour.
- Management needed to understand whether membership customers generated additional value.
- High-value customers needed better retention monitoring.

The goal was to transform transaction data into actionable business recommendations.

# Key Business Questions

## 1. Staffing Optimisation

### Business Question

Are staffing schedules aligned with actual customer demand?

### Analysis

The analysis reviewed:

- Transaction volume by time period
- Hourly customer traffic
- Revenue patterns throughout the day
- Customer ratings by trading period
- Differences between locations

### Key Findings

- Evening recorded the highest overall customer activity.
- Chicago processed the highest transaction volume.
- Revenue generally followed customer demand.
- Peak periods differed slightly between locations.

### Recommendation

Create branch-specific staffing schedules based on actual customer behaviour instead of applying one standard rota across all stores.

---

# 2. Membership Programme Effectiveness

## Business Question

Does the membership programme create additional customer value?

### Analysis

Compared:

- Total revenue generated by members vs non-members
- Average transaction value
- Payment behaviour
- Purchase frequency

### Key Findings

- Members generated £335.3M revenue.
- Normal customers generated £331.5M revenue.
- Average spending per transaction was almost identical.
- Members showed slightly higher purchase frequency.

### Recommendation

Focus membership strategy on retention and repeat purchases rather than increasing average basket size.

---

# 3. Marketing Efficiency

## Business Question

Are marketing campaigns aligned with customer purchasing behaviour?

### Analysis

Reviewed:

- Product preferences by location
- Customer purchasing patterns
- Product category performance
- Demographic differences

### Key Findings

- Product preferences differed between cities.
- Purchasing behaviour was balanced across genders.
- Location had a stronger influence on buying behaviour than demographics.

### Recommendation

Develop location-specific marketing campaigns instead of using one national strategy.

---

# 4. Premium Customer Experience

## Business Question

Are high-value customers at risk?

### Analysis

Analysed:

- Relationship between spending and satisfaction
- Customer ratings
- Premium customer transactions
- Revenue exposure

### Key Findings

- Spending and satisfaction showed almost no relationship.
- Correlation between spending and rating: -0.043.
- 1,450 premium-risk transactions were identified.
- These represented approximately £99.3M revenue exposure.

### Recommendation

Introduce proactive retention strategies for premium customers through personalised engagement and service recovery.
